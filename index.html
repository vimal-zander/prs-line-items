Cross-customer swaps

A swap is one product short on one invoice and excess on another. The two invoices go to different customers, so a physical mix-up between trays explains both complaints at once — and neither one is a counting mistake. Pairs are matched on product, then scored on batch number, quantity, checker, store and (depending on the mode below) invoice date or how far apart the dates are. This always looks across every loaded file, not just the one selected in the date filter above.
